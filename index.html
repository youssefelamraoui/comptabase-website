Core Architecture

Rigorous double-entry accounting engineered for accuracy

ComptaBase is being developed to provide Moroccan accounting professionals and SMEs with a reliable, responsive double-entry bookkeeping engine designed around verified journal practices.

Multi-Journal Architecture & Vouchers

Systematic organization across dedicated subsidiary journals (Purchases, Sales, Bank, Cash, and Miscellaneous Operations). Every entry is linked to an auditable supporting voucher.

Moroccan Accounting Plan (PCGM)

Built to structure chart of accounts according to the Moroccan General Accounting Plan (classes 1 through 8), supporting custom sub-accounts and auxiliary third-party ledgers.

Debit/Credit Balance Controls

Architected with real-time arithmetic balance verification before posting. Designed to prevent unbalanced journal entries from committing to the General Ledger.

Operational Capabilities

Workflows designed for accounting practitioners

From daily invoice registration to periodic closing preparation, ComptaBase focuses on optimizing high-frequency accounting tasks.

  • 1

    Fast Entry & Voucher Templates

    Keyboard-centric interface designed for rapid data input without mouse reliance. Quick counterparty selection and automatic tax calculation according to transaction parameters.

  • 2

    Auxiliary Accounts & Lettering (Lettrage)

    Sub-ledger tracking for accounts 4411 (Suppliers) and 3421 (Customers). Manual and semi-automated lettering to match issued invoices with corresponding payments.

  • 3

    Bank Reconciliation Support

    Matching cash and bank journal entries against official bank statements to isolate timing differences, bank fees, and outstanding entries.

  • 4

    Periodic Statements & Closing Preparation

    Generating the General Ledger, 4-column and 6-column Trial Balances, and Auxiliary Balances to assist in periodic fiscal dossier assembly.

AI Concept & Research

AI assistance as an operator copilot, under strict human control

Artificial intelligence must not replace the professional judgment of qualified accountants. In ComptaBase, AI models are designed solely to reduce repetitive data-entry friction while submitting every suggestion to explicit human review.

Planned Document Processing Pipeline

From scanned document to structured entry suggestion

Prototyping Phase
Step 1 • OCR & Document Vision

Key Field Extraction

Optical extraction of total amounts, dates, invoice numbers, and tax identification metadata (ICE, IF).

Step 2 • Context & Accounting Rules

PCGM Account Suggestion

Recommending expense (Class 6) or revenue (Class 7) accounts based on supplier history and PCGM structure.

Step 3 • Mandatory Gatekeeper

Human Verification

The operator verifies, edits, or rejects suggestions. No journal entry is committed to the ledger without explicit user approval.

Professional Responsibility Notice: ComptaBase does not provide autonomous tax, legal, or financial advice. All assistance models serve strictly as data-entry accelerators and reconciliation aids. Final responsibility for accounting accuracy and statutory compliance remains entirely with qualified human operators.

Regional Specialization

Engineered for the Moroccan business landscape

Generic global tools often miss local legal conventions. ComptaBase incorporates Moroccan accounting requirements directly into its core design.

PCGM Standards

Moroccan Accounting Plan

Structured around the Moroccan General Accounting Plan (Classes 1 to 8) as defined by the Conseil National de la Comptabilité (CNC).

VAT / CGI Parameters

VAT Regimes & Logic

Support for Collections (Encaissements) and Accruals (Débits) VAT regimes, distinction between recoverable VAT on charges (account 34552) and fixed assets (34551), and deduction prorata logic. Note: Applicable VAT rates vary according to transaction classification and current provisions of the Moroccan General Tax Code (Code Général des Impôts - CGI). System tax parameters require verification against prevailing fiscal legislation.

ICE • IF • RC • CNSS

Regulatory Identifiers

First-class fields for Moroccan enterprise identifiers: Common Company Identifier (ICE), Tax Identification (IF), Commercial Registry (RC), and CNSS numbers on third-party accounts.

French / Arabic

Ergonomics for Local Teams

Designed for local business ergonomics with support for French and Arabic account labels and reporting descriptors across Moroccan SMEs.
